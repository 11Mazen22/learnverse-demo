## Open WebUI

Noata AI's chat workspace includes a React/Next.js adaptation of user-interface
structure and interaction patterns from Open WebUI v0.6.5, including concepts
from its Sidebar, chat Navbar/ModelSelector, Messages, MessageInput, and
Controls/Settings components.

Source: https://github.com/open-webui/open-webui/tree/v0.6.5
